Our Services

From rescue to rehoming — what we do, and how you can adopt.

What We Offer

Rescue & Rehabilitation

We rescue abandoned, abused and injured animals, and provide full veterinary care, sterilisation and rehabilitation.

Adoption & Rehoming

We match every animal with a carefully screened forever home. Meet-and-greets ensure the best fit.

Foster Care Programme

Foster families give animals a safe, loving home environment while they wait for adoption.

How Adoption Works

  1. Browse & enquire: Find a pet you connect with and submit an enquiry.
  2. Meet & greet: We arrange a meeting at our centre or via a foster home.
  3. Home check: A volunteer visits your home to ensure a safe environment.
  4. Adoption fee: A contribution covers sterilisation, vaccination and microchipping.
